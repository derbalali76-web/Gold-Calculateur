**Full description (≤4000):**
Gold Calculateur is a professional calculator for jewellers and gold-industry professionals. In seconds it tells you how to bring a group of ingots to the karat you want.

Type ingot weights and karats straight into one table and the total weight and the weighted average karat update instantly. Then pick the method:

• Add ingot — finds the weight of the ingot to add (karat 750, 875, 917, 1000 or any custom karat) to raise or lower the group to your target.
• Cut & refine — finds the part to cut from one ingot, refine to pure gold and return to the group to reach the target karat; the ingot needing the smallest cut is chosen automatically.

Note: results are theoretical and depend on the accuracy of the weights, refining efficiency and the purity of the added ingot.
